02 / Snapshot

Project Overview

The Laura Sanchez identity places the artist at the center. The mark functions as both signature and symbol, rooted in nature, personal expression, and visual warmth.

Overall Concept

The identity integrates a tulip symbol directly into the artist’s name. Paired with expressive typography, the system bridges personal artistry and the organic qualities of growth, renewal, and beauty.

Artist Representation

Using “Laura Sanchez” as the primary mark keeps the artist at the heart of the brand. Her name becomes more than a signature — it represents her voice, vision, and creative presence.

Key Components

  • Wordmark: Alverata Informal
  • Logomark: Tulip “U” symbol
  • Secondary type: Lato for clarity
  • Palette: Soft black, gold, airy blue, floral pink

03 / Logo System

Wordmark & Logomark

The primary logo is built to work first in one color for maximum versatility. Color executions become alternate moments for controlled backgrounds and expressive brand applications.

Primary One-Color Wordmark

The wordmark uses Alverata Informal for an expressive, handcrafted voice. The one-color execution is the default for reliable reproduction across print, stamps, embroidery, and small-scale use.

Tulip “U” Logomark

A standalone mark for icons, stamps, patterns, and smaller brand moments.

Full-Color Wordmark

A color-forward version for feature moments, digital highlights, and controlled backgrounds.

Stacked Lockup

A compact option for avatars, stamps, vertical layouts, and small placements.

Logo Usage Notes

  • Primary: Default to the one-color wordmark for consistent reproduction.
  • Secondary: Use the tulip “U” as an icon, stamp, pattern, or small-space cue.
  • Alternative: Use the full-color wordmark when background and production support it.
  • Do not: Distort, skew, outline, or add heavy effects.

04 / Typography

Typeface System

Laura Sanchez pairs the expressive elegance of Alverata Informal with the clean versatility of Lato, balancing character and readability.

Primary Typeface

Expressive, handcrafted warmth for the wordmark and key brand moments.

Laura Sanchez Aa Bb Cc — 0123456789

Secondary Typeface

Lato supports body copy and informational text with modern simplicity and excellent legibility.

Clear, confident, and readable. Aa Bb Cc — 0123456789

Typography Roles

  • Alverata Informal: Wordmark, headlines, signature moments
  • Lato: Body copy, captions, supporting text, digital UI
  • System rule: Personality first, clarity always
